How to use

Bring Homer postcards to life

Go from blank NFC cardstock to an embedded story in a few steps.

  1. Order official Homer NFC postcard kits from shop.homer.cards.
  2. Launch the Homer builder on desktop (this prototype) and sign in with your account.
  3. Attach photos, videos, or links, pick authentication, and review the preview.
  4. Tap “Embed to Postcard” and hold the blank card near the NFC tag to encode your message.
  5. Drop the physical postcard in the mail so recipients can tap and unlock it.

Need help? Ping support@homer.cards and we’ll walk you through tagging your first card.
